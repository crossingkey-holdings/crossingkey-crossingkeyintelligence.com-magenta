# Documentation, Receipts, and Release Rules

- Separate facts, tests, inferences, plans, and opinions. Never turn a plan
  into a completion claim.
- Significant work records its target, source files, source/license status,
  commands/tests, date, known limits, rollback path, and approval boundary.
- Use stable filenames and human-readable Markdown. Do not embed secrets,
  private identifiers, private URLs, or customer data.
- For public or paid documentation, record the exact version and scope of the
  accompanying asset. Do not promise a download that does not exist.
- When an official source changes or deprecates a workflow, update or withdraw
  affected public guidance rather than leaving stale instructions live.
