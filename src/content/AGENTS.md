# Public Documentation and Content Rules

## Public-safe material only

- Publish only material classified `PUBLIC_SAFE`. Private CrossingKey methods,
  raw ledgers, unpublished systems, secrets, client material, and unreleased
  creative IP stay out of this directory.
- Every factual technical article needs primary-source links and an access date.
  Explain scope and limitations in plain language.
- Use the fields `title`, `slug`, `category`, `summary`, `publicStatus`,
  `updatedAt`, `sources`, and `claimsStatus` for new content records.

## Writing standard

- Teach one concrete outcome per article: what it is, why it matters, a safe
  first action, common failure mode, and next step.
- Be direct and useful; do not use vague “hidden secrets,” untestable claims,
  scraped/copied articles, fake expertise, or promises of income.
- Free articles teach concepts and safe starting points. Paid products may
  include maintained templates, checklists, examples, and implementation packs
  only after those files exist and are verified.
- Describe agents as controlled tool-using workflows, not autonomous people.
- State that prompts guide a request; they do not secretly retrain provider
  models. Explain fine-tuning only from current official documentation.

## Citation record

For material claims, include canonical URL, publisher, access date, short
paraphrase, and `OBSERVED`/`TESTED`/`INFERRED`/`PLANNED` status. If a source is
not primary, name it as supplemental and identify the primary source it needs
to be checked against.
